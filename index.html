Open MSME is a catalog — and a build roadmap — of software for micro, small and medium businesses in India and the Philippines. The ideas here start from specific, verifiable problems: a GST job-work deadline a spreadsheet cannot compute, a Philippine BIR receipt rule, the 45-day MSME payment law, a cold-chain log that has to stand up in a dispute. The first 5 tools are live and free to use right now; the rest of the first wave is being built, and every tool ships as its own open-source repository with its own explainer site.
