MSME-017

FPO Pooling & Split-Payment Ledger

Idea

Problem. When an FPO or coop aggregates members' produce and sells it in bulk, tying each member's contributed quantity and quality to their share of the final payout is a reconciliation nightmare done on paper slips.

Who it helps. FPO/coop collection-center operators and their members

What it does. A mobile app that records each member's deposit (quantity, grade, moisture) at the collection center, pools lots for sale, then splits net proceeds back by contribution minus shared costs, with a per-member passbook.

Regulation. Producer Company norms (IN), CDA/RA 9520 (PH), e-invoice on bulk sale

MSME-637

Agri-Drone Spray Service Log & Billing

Idea

Problem. Operators spray dozens of fields a day for hire but track sorties, chemical used, area and payments on paper; they cannot prove sprayed acreage to a farmer who disputes the bill, and they keep nothing like the sortie/chemical log that DGCA and agri-department subsidy audits expect.

Who it helps. Independent kisan-drone spray operators and drone entrepreneurs (including Namo Drone Didi SHGs) who spray farmers' fields on hire.

What it does. A field app that starts a sortie, captures the GPS-walked field boundary and sprayed area, the registered agrochemical, dose and tank mixes, battery-per-acre and the farmer, then produces a per-field per-acre bill plus a running sortie-and-chemical logbook ready for DGCA/subsidy records; works offline and syncs later.

Regulation. DGCA UAS Rules 2021 / CIB&RC drone-application SOP; SMAM agri-drone subsidy records

MSME-638

Poultry Batch FCR & Contract-Grower Settlement Ledger

Idea

Problem. A grower's profit lives or dies on feed conversion and mortality, but they reconcile chick placement, daily feed bags, mortality and the integrator's settlement sheet by hand - and rarely catch when the integrator mis-states FCR, feed recovery or the growing charge.

Who it helps. Independent broiler/layer farmers and small contract-growers tied to integrators (NECC/private).

What it does. Record placement (chicks, breed), daily feed, mortality and weights per batch, auto-compute live FCR, mortality % and batch P&L, then reconstruct the contract settlement (feed-cost recovery, growing charge per kg, FCR incentive/penalty) so the grower can check the integrator's payout to the last rupee or peso.

MSME-639

Aquaculture Pond Feed & Water-Quality Log

Idea

Problem. Pond outcomes turn on feed discipline and water parameters, and antibiotic misuse gets whole export lots rejected - yet farmers keep feed, dissolved-oxygen/pH/salinity and any medication on scraps of paper, with no withdrawal-period record a processor or authority will accept.

Who it helps. Small shrimp/fish pond farmers and lease operators (Andhra/Odisha; BFAR-registered ponds in the Philippines).

What it does. A pond-side offline app to log daily feed, DO/pH/salinity/ammonia, water exchange, inputs and any antibiotic with its withdrawal clock, per pond per crop; it warns when a planned harvest falls inside a withdrawal period and exports a per-pond input-and-residue history for the processor/CAA audit.

Regulation. Coastal Aquaculture Authority (CAA) registration (IN) / BFAR registration (PH); CAA antibiotic ban & seafood-export MRL

MSME-640

Sugarcane Supply-Ticket & FRP Payment Tracker

Idea

Problem. A grower delivers cane all season against mill permits/calendars, but reconciling each supply ticket's weight, the mill's running account and whether the Fair & Remunerative Price was paid inside the 14-day legal window is near-impossible on paper - and overdue-payment interest claims go unmade.

Who it helps. Cane-growing farmers and village cane-supply secretaries delivering to sugar mills.

What it does. Record each cane-supply permit and delivery (gross/tare/net, variety), accrue the amount due at the notified FRP/SAP, match mill payments against it, and flag any supply unpaid past 14 days with the interest then due - producing a clean grower-vs-mill statement.

Regulation. Sugarcane (Control) Order 1966 - FRP + 14-day payment & interest
