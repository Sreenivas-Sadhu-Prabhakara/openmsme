MSME-044

RAbill — Running-Account Bill Generator

Idea

Problem. Contractors bill clients progressively but prepare RA bills (previous vs up-to-date quantities, retention, recoveries, tax) by hand, causing disputes and delayed payment.

Who it helps. civil contractors, sub-contractors, PMCs

What it does. Go app: load BOQ items + cumulative measured quantity, generates an RA bill (previous/present/up-to-date columns), retention %, mobilization-advance recovery, TDS/EWT deduction and net payable as a PDF.

Regulation. TDS 194C (IN), EWT 2% (PH BIR), retention / defect-liability norms
