MSME-038

Living-Wage Craft Pricing Calculator

Live

Problem. Artisans chronically underprice: they count materials but omit their own labour hours, skill premium, wastage, and overhead, then sell near cost and stay poor.

Who it helps. Individual artisans, SHG leaders, and D2C craft founders setting prices.

What it does. Enter materials, hours, a chosen fair hourly wage (benchmarked to regional minimum/living wage), wastage, and overhead to get costed retail, wholesale, and export prices with the margin shown.

Regulation. Regional minimum-wage schedules (IN state boards / PH DOLE regional wage orders) as the wage floor reference.

MSME-678

PM Vishwakarma Enrollment & Benefit Tracker

Idea

Problem. PM Vishwakarma is a trade-eligibility gate followed by a multi-stage benefit chain: certificate+ID, 5-7 day basic skilling with a Rs500/day stipend, a Rs15,000 toolkit e-voucher, a Rs1 lakh then Rs2 lakh collateral-free loan at 5%, and a Rs1/transaction digital incentive. Artisans never know which stage they are at, what unlocks next, or why a benefit has stalled.

Who it helps. Individual artisans in the 18 PM Vishwakarma trades (potters, carpenters, blacksmiths, goldsmiths, cobblers, basket/mat weavers, toy-makers, etc.) and the CSC VLEs or NGO facilitators who enroll them.

What it does. A per-artisan enrollment-to-benefit pipeline: a trade-eligibility and exclusion check (e.g. income-tax payers and govt employees are out), a document pre-fill for the CSC biometric enrollment, then a stage tracker recording certificate/ID issue, skilling completion, toolkit-voucher redemption, each loan tranche, and digital-transaction incentive accrual, surfacing the next action and missing document for every artisan.

Regulation. PM Vishwakarma Scheme (Ministry of MSME)

MSME-679

BIS Hallmarking (HUID) Register for Small Jewelers

Idea

Problem. Under the HUID mandate every gold article sold must carry a 6-digit HUID and only hallmarked stock is legal, yet small jewelers juggle items sent to the Assaying & Hallmarking Centre (AHC), items returned with HUIDs, and legacy non-HUID stock with no per-piece record for BIS market-sampling or a consumer complaint.

Who it helps. Small goldsmiths, karigars and single-counter jewelers operating in mandatory-hallmarking districts.

What it does. A per-piece hallmarking register: log each article with purity (14/18/20/22/23/24K), weight and photo, group them into AHC submission lots, record the HUID and hallmark returned, flag hallmarked-vs-pending-vs-legacy stock, and keep the trail BIS or a customer complaint would demand, including the purity/caratage disclosure at point of sale.

Regulation. BIS Act 2016 + Hallmarking Regulations (mandatory HUID)

MSME-680

Precious-Material Issue-&-Return Weight Ledger

Idea

Problem. Gold, silver and zari thread are issued to karigars by weight and must come back as finished weight plus scrap within an allowed wastage, but without a tight weight ledger shortage and pilferage hide inside 'wastage' and disputes with karigars become unresolvable.

Who it helps. Jewelry karkhanas, goldsmith and silversmith units, and zari/zardozi workshops that hand out precious material to karigars.

What it does. An issue-and-return weight ledger: record material issued to each karigar by weight and purity, the finished-piece weight and returned scrap, the agreed wastage percentage, and auto-compute the shortage/excess per job and per karigar, with a running balance of material out with each worker.

Idea
