MSME-042

Verifiable Skill Certificate Issuer + Public Verify

First wave

Problem. Certificates are plain PDFs anyone can forge, so employers distrust them and institutes get no credit for genuine training.

Who it helps. Skilling providers, coaching centres and TVET centres issuing completion or competency certificates.

What it does. Issue cryptographically signed certificates (QR plus optional W3C Verifiable Credential) with a free public verify page that confirms authenticity, course and date without contacting the institute.

Regulation. DigiLocker/NAD alignment (IN); TESDA certificate integrity (PH).

MSME-719

Education-Services GST Exemption & Fee-Invoice Classifier

Idea

Problem. Education is a GST minefield: school education up to higher secondary is exempt, approved vocational/skill courses can be exempt, but coaching and most private training are taxable at 18%; owners either wrongly skip GST and get notices, or wrongly charge it, and their receipts are non-compliant.

Who it helps. Coaching centres, skilling providers, pre-schools and training institutes unsure whether their fees attract GST.

What it does. A short decision tool that classifies each fee head (school tuition, coaching, approved vocational course, hostel/mess, books, transport) as exempt or taxable with the reasoning and notification reference, computes the GST, and generates a compliant fee invoice/receipt template with the right HSN/SAC and exemption notes.

Regulation. CGST exemption Notification 12/2017-CT(R) entry 66 (educational institutions / approved vocational education); 18% GST on coaching
