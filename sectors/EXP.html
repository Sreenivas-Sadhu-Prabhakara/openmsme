MSME-022

Export Price Builder (EXW to DDP)

Live

Problem. Exporters set a USD price by gut and discover too late that freight, insurance, bank charges and port fees erased the margin, or they lose deals by padding blindly.

Who it helps. Micro and small exporters preparing their first quotations.

What it does. Builds price up from ex-works cost through inland haulage, THC, freight, insurance (CIF+10%), bank/LC charges and target margin, then outputs the correct number for each Incoterm (FOB/CFR/CIF/DDP) with a clean shareable breakdown.

Regulation. Incoterms 2020; marine-insurance CIF+10% convention.

MSME-653

EPCG & Advance Authorisation Export-Obligation & EODC Tracker

Idea

Problem. An EPCG licence carries a 6x-duty-saved export obligation over 6 years (average plus specific EO), and Advance Authorisation ties duty-free inputs to a matching export quantity under SION norms; MSMEs lose track of how much EO is left, miss block-wise deadlines, and face duty plus 15% interest and penalty when they cannot produce an EODC at redemption.

Who it helps. Small manufacturer-exporters who imported machinery under EPCG or inputs under Advance Authorisation against an export obligation.

What it does. Register each licence with its duty saved, EO value/quantity, blocks and validity; log every export shipping bill against it (with the licence number endorsed), auto-compute average and specific EO fulfilled vs balance, alert before each block and the final date, and assemble the EODC/redemption file (shipping bills, BRCs, installation certificate) for DGFT.

Regulation. EPCG Scheme & Advance Authorisation (FTP 2023); Export Obligation; EODC; SION

MSME-654

Merchant Export 0.1% GST Concessional-Procurement Tracker

Idea

Problem. Notification 40/2017-CT(R) and 41/2017-IGST(R) let a merchant exporter procure at 0.1% GST, but only if strict conditions hold - order placed on the supplier's GSTIN with a copy to the supplier's jurisdictional officer, goods moved directly to port/ICD, and export completed within 90 days of the supplier's tax invoice; miss any and the concession is denied with interest.

Who it helps. Merchant exporters buying finished goods from domestic suppliers for export.

What it does. For each 0.1% procurement, generate the compliant purchase order and the intimation to the jurisdictional officer, start a 90-day export clock, link the shipping bill/EGM proof when exported, and flag any procurement nearing day 90 without an export against it.

Regulation. Notification 40/2017-Central Tax (Rate); 41/2017-Integrated Tax (Rate)

MSME-655

PH Export-Sales VAT Zero-Rating Substantiation & Refund Pack

Idea

Problem. Export sales are zero-rated under NIRC Sec 106(A)(2)/108(B), but to defend the 0% in a BIR audit and claim a refund of input VAT under Sec 112, the exporter must prove the export with a matched set - sales invoice, export declaration/bill of lading, and proof of inward foreign-currency remittance - which MSMEs assemble haphazardly and then lose the refund.

Who it helps. Philippine exporters whose sales are VAT zero-rated and who accumulate input VAT.

What it does. Build one evidence bundle per export sale (zero-rated sales invoice, export declaration, BL/AWB, bank inward-remittance credit), reconcile the peso amounts across them, compute the input VAT attributable to zero-rated sales, and output the Sec 112 refund application packet with its substantiation schedule.

Regulation. NIRC Sec 106(A)(2), 108(B) (zero-rating) and Sec 112 (VAT refund)

MSME-656

Pre-Shipment Inspection & Certificate-of-Conformity Coordinator

Idea

Problem. Many destinations block goods at the port of entry without a pre-shipment Certificate of Conformity - Nigeria's SONCAP, Kenya's PVoC, Saudi SABER/SASO and similar - each with its own route (product registration, test reports, physical inspection); MSMEs discover the requirement only after the buyer's goods are stuck and pay demurrage.

Who it helps. Exporters shipping to African and Middle-East markets that enforce destination conformity assessment.

What it does. Enter product (HS) and destination to get the exact scheme, route (registration vs batch inspection vs licensing), required test reports and accredited bodies; then manage the file per shipment - book the inspection, upload test reports, and track issuance of the CoC before the vessel sails.

Regulation. SONCAP (Nigeria); PVoC (Kenya, KEBS); SABER/SASO (Saudi Arabia)

Idea

MSME-658

Packing Credit & Interest-Equalisation Export-Finance Tracker

Idea

Problem. Pre-shipment packing credit (and PCFC) is advanced against confirmed orders/LCs and must be liquidated out of export proceeds within the sanctioned period; MSMEs lose track of drawing power against each order, risk overdue liquidation (which converts to costly ordinary interest), and fail to claim the Interest Equalisation Scheme subvention they are entitled to.

Who it helps. Small exporters funding production with pre- and post-shipment export credit.

What it does. Track each packing-credit drawing against its order/LC, monitor drawing power and the liquidation deadline, match export proceeds/BRCs to liquidate the right advance, and compute the IES interest subvention due per account for the bank claim.

Regulation. RBI export credit (packing credit/PCFC); Interest Equalisation Scheme (IES)
