Idea

MSME-051

PM Surya Ghar Solar Payback Calculator

Idea

Problem. Homeowners and small installers cannot quickly turn a bill into a system size, generation estimate, the PM Surya Ghar subsidy slab, and payback, so warm leads go cold.

Who it helps. Rooftop solar installers (as a lead magnet) and homeowners

What it does. Static calculator: bill to kW size to annual kWh by state irradiance to the PM Surya Ghar subsidy slab (30k per kW up to 3 kW) to net cost and payback, emitting a one-page proposal.

Regulation. PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, DISCOM net-metering

Idea
