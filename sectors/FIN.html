MSME-033

GSTR-2B vs Purchase ITC Reconciler

Live

Problem. MSMEs silently lose input tax credit (real cash) when suppliers don't file or file wrong, and mismatches between GSTR-2B and the purchase register go unnoticed until too late.

Who it helps. GST-registered MSMEs and their accountants

What it does. Upload GSTR-2B JSON + the purchase register; the tool matches invoice-by-invoice, flags missing or mismatched ITC, totals the blocked cash, and drafts supplier follow-ups to recover it.

MSME-664

Rule 37 180-Day ITC-Reversal Guard

Idea

Problem. If you don't pay a supplier within 180 days of their invoice, the ITC you already claimed must be reversed with interest. Buyers forget, get caught in audit, and pay interest and penalty on credits they'd already used, a different law and threshold from the 43B(h) 45-day supplier rule.

Who it helps. GST-registered MSMEs that claim ITC on credit purchases

What it does. Ingest the purchase register plus payments made; per invoice it runs the 180-day clock from invoice date, nets part-payments, flags invoices nearing day 180 with the ITC at risk and the interest if missed, and either prompts a payment or stages the exact reversal entry (and re-claim once paid).

Regulation. CGST Rule 37; Sec 16(2) second proviso (180-day payment condition)

MSME-667

Idea

Regulation. RBI CMA / Maximum Permissible Bank Finance (Tandon-Nayak norms)

MSME-668

Drawing Power & Monthly Stock-Statement Generator

Idea

Problem. CC/OD borrowers must submit a monthly stock and book-debt statement; drawing power is (paid stock + eligible debtors - creditors) less the bank's margin. Get it wrong and the account is flagged irregular; do it by hand monthly and it eats a day.

Who it helps. MSMEs running a cash-credit (CC) or overdraft limit against stock and book debts

What it does. Enter or import stock, debtors (with aging cut-off) and creditors; it applies the sanction's margins, excludes slow/over-90-day debtors and unpaid stock, computes drawing power vs sanctioned limit, warns if utilisation will breach DP, and prints the bank's stock-statement format.

Regulation. RBI cash-credit drawing-power / stock & book-debt statement norms
