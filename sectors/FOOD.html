MSME-008

FSSAI License Navigator

First wave

Problem. Food MSMEs don't know whether they need Basic Registration, a State License, or a Central License (driven by turnover, capacity, multi-state and exports), and routinely let licenses lapse, triggering penalties up to lakhs and shutdowns.

Who it helps. Street vendors, home kitchens, small manufacturers, kirana-to-FMCG units applying for or renewing an FSSAI licence.

What it does. A 60-second decision-tree that takes turnover, activity, capacity, states and export status and outputs the exact licence tier, document checklist, govt fee, and an expiry watchdog that nags before renewal.

Regulation. FSSAI (FoSCoS) Schedule 1/2 licensing tiers; renewal rules.

MSME-009

Compliant Food Label Studio

Idea

Problem. Packaged-food MSMEs get labels rejected or fined for missing mandatory declarations: ingredients in descending order, bold allergens, veg/non-veg symbol, FSSAI logo+licence no., net quantity, MRP, date marking and nutrition panel.

Who it helps. Spice, snack, pickle and bakery units packing for retail; PH processed-food SMEs needing FDA-compliant labels.

What it does. A form-driven generator that produces a print-ready label and lints it: validates descending-order ingredients, allergen bolding, net-qty format and symbol placement, with an FSSAI-2020 / PH-FDA rule toggle.

Regulation. FSSAI Labelling & Display 2020, Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules; PH FDA AO 2014-0030.

MSME-617

FSSAI Annual Return (D1/D2) Filer

Idea

Problem. Every FSSAI-licensed manufacturer must file Form D1 by 31 May (D2 half-yearly for milk) declaring product-wise, packaging-wise quantities handled; the penalty for missing it is Rs 100/day accruing indefinitely and it blocks renewal, yet most owners never knew the return existed.

Who it helps. Licensed food manufacturers, importers and 100%-export units (plus milk/dairy units for Form D2).

What it does. Pull the year's production/sales into product- and packaging-wise quantities, validate them against the licence's product categories, output the exact D1/D2 figures ready to key into FoSCoS, and run a running late-fee meter plus a May-deadline watchdog across every licence held.

Regulation. FSSAI FSS (Licensing & Registration) Regulations Form D1/D2 annual return

MSME-618

LMPC Packaged-Commodity Declaration & Registration Helper

Idea

Problem. Beyond the FSSAI label, the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules mandate a separate set of declarations - MRP inclusive of all taxes, standard net-quantity format, unit sale price, month/year of packing, packer/importer identity and minimum character heights - plus LMPC registration; metrology inspectors fine MSMEs who got only the FSSAI label right.

Who it helps. Packers, manufacturers and importers of any pre-packaged food sold by weight, volume or number.

What it does. A metrology-specific declaration builder that lays out the principal display panel fields, enforces net-quantity rounding and font-height rules, computes and prints unit sale price, and assembles the LMPC/importer-registration pack with the fee and jurisdiction.

Regulation. Legal Metrology Act 2009 & Packaged Commodities Rules 2011; LMPC registration

MSME-620

Fryer-Oil TPM & Used-Cooking-Oil (RUCO) Log

Idea

Problem. FSSAI bars using frying oil once Total Polar Compounds exceed 25%, and larger users must hand spent oil to registered UCO aggregators under RUCO - but kitchens keep no record of when oil crossed the limit or where it went, failing inspection and forfeiting the biodiesel rebate.

Who it helps. QSRs, snack and namkeen fryers, sweet shops and any high-volume frying kitchen.

What it does. Log each fryer's oil top-up and TPM test reading against the 25% limit with a change-now alert, and record every used-oil pickup (quantity, aggregator, receipt) to build the RUCO disposal trail and an auditable oil-life report.

Regulation. FSSAI Total Polar Compounds 25% frying-oil limit; RUCO / UCO disposal to registered aggregators

MSME-625

Food-Handler Health Card & FoSTaC Supervisor Tracker

Idea

Problem. Schedule 4 requires annual medical fitness and inoculation for every food handler, and FoSTaC requires one trained Food Safety Supervisor per 25 handlers; PH city health offices require a current food-handler's health certificate per worker - all kept on paper and routinely lapsed at inspection.

Who it helps. Any food business employing food handlers - restaurants, caterers, cloud kitchens, processing units.

What it does. A staff roster storing each handler's medical-fitness date, vaccination and health card/certificate, computing the trained-supervisor-to-handler ratio live and firing escalating reminders before any card or FoSTaC certificate expires, with a printable compliance board for the inspector.

Regulation. FSSAI Schedule 4 medical fitness + FoSTaC Food Safety Supervisor (1:25); PH LGU food-handler health certificate
