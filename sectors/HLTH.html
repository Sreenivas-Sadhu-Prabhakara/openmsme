MSME-039

Schedule H1 Sale Register

Live

Problem. Pharmacies are legally required to keep a separate 3-year register of Schedule H1 drug sales (antibiotics, habit-forming drugs) with prescriber and patient details, but most keep messy paper books that fail drug-inspector audits.

Who it helps. Retail pharmacy owners and registered pharmacists

What it does. Single-binary app that logs each H1 sale (drug, batch, qty, prescriber reg no, patient) in the exact statutory field set, enforces 3-year retention, and prints the inspector-ready bound-register format with search.

Regulation. Drugs & Cosmetics Rules, Schedule H1 register

MSME-692

PCPNDT Form F Register & Monthly Filing

Idea

Problem. Every obstetric scan legally requires a completed Form F with referrer, indication and signed declarations; one missing or inconsistent field can mean the machine is sealed and the sonologist prosecuted. Clinics keep these as loose paper, dread the quarterly inspection, and miss the monthly report to the District Appropriate Authority.

Who it helps. Ultrasound clinics, radiology/sonography centers and imaging-equipped OB-GYN practices

What it does. A guided Form F for each scan that enforces every statutory field, links the referring doctor and indication list, captures declarations and the sonologist's sign-off, stores records for the mandated retention, and auto-compiles the month's Form F count into the report format the Appropriate Authority expects, with a one-click inspection pack.

Regulation. PC-PNDT Act 1994 (Form F, monthly report to Appropriate Authority, record retention)

MSME-694

Idea

MSME-698

Senior/PWD Medicine Discount Till & Purchase Booklet Register

Idea

Problem. Pharmacies must give senior citizens and PWDs 20% off plus VAT exemption on medicines, record each sale in the buyer's purchase booklet with their OSCA/PWD ID, and keep the separate register that supports the 20% as a BIR tax deduction; done by hand it is slow, error-prone and leaves money unclaimed.

Regulation. RA 9994 (Expanded Senior Citizens Act) & RA 10754 (PWD): 20% + VAT exemption, BIR deduction
