MSME-018

Fleet Document Expiry Vault

Live

Problem. Every truck carries RC, insurance, PUC, fitness, national/state permit, road tax and the driver's DL, each expiring on a different date; one lapse means a seized vehicle and a fat fine.

Who it helps. Fleet owners and transport managers running 2-50 vehicles.

What it does. Per-vehicle document locker with all expiry dates, scanned copies, WhatsApp/SMS reminders at 30/15/3 days, a renewal checklist, and VAHAN/Parivahan/SARATHI deep-links to action the renewal.

Regulation. Motor Vehicles Act documents (RC, insurance, PUC, fitness, permit, road tax, DL), VAHAN/Parivahan/SARATHI.

MSME-642

Axle-Load & Overload Penalty Calculator

Live

Problem. Loading past the registered gross/axle weight triggers steep penalties and mandatory offloading at check-posts and weighbridges (Section 194 in India, anti-overloading charges in the Philippines), yet the person loading rarely knows the remaining safe payload or the fine exposure.

Who it helps. Tipper and truck owners, loaders and dispatchers deciding how much to load.

What it does. Enter the RC/OR-CR gross and axle ratings, unladen weight and the load, and get the remaining safe payload per axle, the overload tonnage, and the penalty exposure under the right regime (IN Sec 194 per-tonne scale, PH anti-overloading), with a clear load/don't-load verdict before dispatch.

Regulation. Motor Vehicles Act 1988 Sec 194 (India); RA 8794 / DPWH anti-overloading (Philippines)

MSME-643

Volumetric Weight & Carton-Cube Optimizer

Live

Problem. Couriers bill the higher of actual and volumetric weight, so an oversized carton quietly inflates every shipment's cost, and sellers also guess how many units fit a carton and how many cartons fit a pallet.

Who it helps. D2C/online sellers, 3PLs and exporters' domestic legs packing parcels for couriers.

What it does. Enter item and carton dimensions to get the volumetric weight, the chargeable weight, the best-fit box from your own box library, units-per-carton and cartons-per-pallet, with an alert when a smaller box would cut the chargeable weight and cost.

MSME-055

Idea

Problem. Fleet owners quote freight on gut feel with no idea of true per-km cost (diesel, driver bata, tyre, EMI, maintenance, tolls), so they underprice trips and quietly bleed cash.

Who it helps. One-to-twenty truck fleet owners and owner-drivers.

Idea

MSME-644

National Permit Multi-State Authorization Tracker

Idea

Problem. A Goods Carriage National Permit needs an annual composite authorization fee and stays valid only in states whose authorization is paid; drivers get detained at check-posts when a state's authorization has lapsed, and owners track the per-state paid-until dates nowhere.

Who it helps. Goods-carriage fleet owners and transport managers running National Permit trucks across state borders.

What it does. Per-truck National Permit register holding the 5-year permit and the annual authorization, plus a state-by-state grid of paid-until dates; reminders before the annual authorization and any planned route's states lapse, with Parivahan/VAHAN payment deep-links and a border-readiness check for a chosen route.

Regulation. Goods Carriage National Permit, CMVR Rule 87 (authorization fee)

MSME-645

Inbound Freight-Bill Audit & 3-Way Match (Rate-LR-POD)

Idea

Problem. Shippers agree lane rates (per-ton/per-km/fixed) but transporters' monthly freight bills quietly diverge (wrong rate, inflated weight, duplicate LR numbers, missing PODs) and staff approve them blind, overpaying and risking ITC on unmatched e-way bills.

Who it helps. Manufacturers, distributors and traders who hire transporters and pay monthly freight bills.

What it does. Load the agreed rate contract per transporter/lane, import or key the freight bills and LRs, and auto 3-way match every bill line to its contracted rate and its delivered LR/POD; flag rate variances, over-weight, duplicate/missing LRs and missing PODs, and emit an approved-for-payment statement listing the disputed lines.

Regulation. GST ITC (freight invoice + e-way bill matching)

MSME-648

Idea

Problem. Loading-unloading labour is paid per bag/per ton/per truck across many jobs a day; tallying who loaded what, computing piece-rate wages and advances, and (in board states) the Mathadi levy and register is all manual and dispute-prone.

Who it helps. Transport godowns, mandis and warehouses that use loading/unloading labour (hamal/mathadi gangs).

Regulation. Maharashtra Mathadi, Hamal and Other Manual Workers Act 1969 (and sister Hamali-board laws)

Idea

Problem. Hazmat road movement requires a TREM (transport emergency) card, a driver with the hazardous-goods endorsement/training, correct class placards and a per-trip document set under CMVR Rules 129-137; any slip means heavy penalty, impounding and a voided insurance claim.

Who it helps. Transporters and tankers carrying dangerous/hazardous goods by road (chemicals, LPG, fuel, paints).

Regulation. Central Motor Vehicles Rules, Rules 129-137 (carriage of dangerous goods); TREM card
