MSME-001

Job-Work ITC-04 Reconciler

First wave

Problem. Goods come in on job-work challans and must return within 1 year (inputs) / 3 years (capital goods) or become a deemed supply with tax liability, and the quarterly ITC-04 return must reconcile every challan out and in. Shops track this in Excel and lose the clock.

Who it helps. GST-registered machining, plating and fabrication job-work shops that receive principals' goods on delivery challan

What it does. A register of inbound/outbound job-work challans per principal with the deemed-supply aging clock, part reconciliation, and one-click ITC-04 JSON export in the GST-portal schema.

Regulation. CGST Sec 143, Rule 45, ITC-04 quarterly return, Udyam

MSME-002

Machining Quote Calculator

Live

MSME-601

Factory Electricity Bill Decoder (Max-Demand & Power-Factor Penalty)

Idea

Problem. Industrial tariffs bill contract demand, recorded maximum demand (with penalty if it exceeds sanctioned load), power-factor incentive/penalty, Time-of-Day slabs and fuel surcharge, all buried in a dense bill. Owners can't tell if they are paying penalties, whether to revise contract demand, or how much a capacitor bank would save.

Who it helps. Small and medium factory owners on HT/LT industrial connections who can't read their own electricity bill and quietly overpay on demand and power-factor penalties.

What it does. Enter the bill's key figures (sanctioned/contract demand, recorded MD, units by ToD, power factor, rate slabs) and it decodes every line, quantifies MD-exceedance and low-PF penalties in rupees, recommends a revised contract demand, sizes the APFC capacitor bank to reach target PF with payback months, and charts a 12-month MD/PF trend.

Regulation. Electricity Act 2003; state DISCOM HT/LT industrial tariff orders (maximum-demand & power-factor clauses)

MSME-602

Sheet-Metal Bend Deduction & Flat-Pattern Calculator

Idea

Problem. Cut the flat blank wrong, ignoring bend allowance/deduction and the K-factor for the material and radius, and every bent part is oversize or undersize: scrap, rework, wasted sheet. Operators guess the blank size or learn by ruining parts.

Who it helps. Press-brake operators and sheet-metal fabricators cutting blanks before bending.

What it does. Enter material, thickness, bend angle(s), inside radius and the flat dimensions; it computes bend allowance/bend deduction with a material/K-factor table, returns the exact flat-blank length and the bend-line positions / back-gauge settings, and saves a per-part bend sheet; works offline on a phone at the machine.

MSME-603

Machine Hour-Rate & Shop-Overhead Costing Calculator

Idea

Problem. Quotes rest on a made-up 'Rs 300/hour' that ignores real depreciation, power, labour, floor, interest and true utilization, so some jobs are quoted below cost and the shop can't see which work actually makes money. The existing quote calculator needs this rate as an input; most shops guess it.

Who it helps. Job-shop owners who quote using a machine rate they have never actually calculated.

What it does. Enter each machine's capital cost, life, power draw and local tariff, assigned and indirect labour, floor area and rent, consumables and expected productive hours; it computes a defensible machine-hour rate per machine and a blended shop rate, shows the full currency build-up, and does break-even utilization, giving a rate you feed straight into quoting.

MSME-604

BIS/ISI QCO Licence & Marking Compliance Tracker

Idea

Problem. QCOs are expanding fast and sweep in shops that never needed ISI. A factory must suddenly hold a valid BIS licence, mark every unit correctly, keep lab test reports current and renew on time, or goods are seized and unsellable. Owners don't know which IS standard applies, when the licence/marking fee lapses, or when the next factory sample test is due.

Who it helps. MSME manufacturers of products newly pulled under mandatory BIS certification by Quality Control Orders (toys, footwear, electrical goods, steel, kitchenware, fasteners).

What it does. Register each product to its IS standard and QCO, store the BIS licence (CM/L number) with validity and annual marking-fee date, schedule the periodic factory sample testing, hold lab reports with expiry, and generate the correct ISI mark spec (standard no. + licence no.) per SKU. Alerts fire before every deadline.

Regulation. BIS Act 2016; product-specific Quality Control Orders (QCO); ISI marking

MSME-605

SPCB Consent (CTE/CTO) & Form V / Hazardous-Waste Returns Tracker

Idea

Problem. A factory must hold a valid Consent to Establish/Operate, file the Environmental Statement (Form V) by 30 September every year, and, if it generates hazardous waste, keep manifests and file the annual return (Form 4) under the 2016 rules. Consents lapse, Form V is forgotten, and each renewal re-assembles the same documents, risking closure notices.

Who it helps. Orange/Red-category MSME factories (engineering, foundry, treatment units) under State Pollution Control Board oversight.

What it does. A compliance cockpit storing CTE/CTO with validity, category and conditions; a returns calendar (Form V by 30 Sep, haz-waste Form 4 by 30 Jun); a Form 10 hazardous-waste manifest log with quantities to the authorized TSDF; a document vault that pre-fills the next renewal pack; and alerts before every due date.

Regulation. Water Act 1974 & Air Act 1981 Consent (CTE/CTO); Environmental Statement Form V; Hazardous & Other Wastes (M&TM) Rules 2016 (Form 4/Form 10)

MSME-606

DOLE OSH Compliance Pack (RA 11058 / DO 198-18)

Idea

Problem. RA 11058 and DO 198-18 require a trained safety officer, an OSH committee with minutes, a company OSH program, mandatory 8-/40-hour training, periodic risk assessment, and reports (Annual Medical Report, Work Accident/Illness Report within set days). Small shops don't learn what's required until an inspection or an accident, then face stiff per-day penalties.

Who it helps. Philippine MSME manufacturers and fabrication shops that must meet DOLE occupational safety and health standards.

What it does. A guided pack sized to the shop's worker count and risk level: generates the required OSH program document, tracks safety-officer and worker training with certificate expiry, logs committee meetings and minutes, maintains the hazard/risk register, and prepares the mandated reports (WAIR, Annual Medical/Exposure) with deadlines and alerts.

Regulation. RA 11058 and DOLE DO 198-18 (OSH Standards); mandatory WAIR and OSH training/reporting
