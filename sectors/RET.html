MSME-015

BoliKhata — Voice Credit Ledger

Live

Problem. Corner-store owners record who-owes-what in paper notebooks or not at all, and most can't type English fast enough to keep a phone app current.

Who it helps. Single-owner kirana (IN) and sari-sari (PH) shopkeepers who extend daily udhaar/pautang to neighbours.

What it does. An offline-first app where the owner taps once and says 'Ramu, 200 rupees, cooking oil' in Hindi/Tamil/Tagalog/Cebuano; on-device speech-to-text posts the ledger entry, running balances sync when signal returns.

Regulation. DPDP Act (IN) / Data Privacy Act (PH) consent for debtor phone numbers; no financial licence needed (informal credit record only).

MSME-631

Jewellery Stock + HUID & Making-Charge Biller

Idea

Problem. Each bill is a manual sum of live metal rate times net weight plus making charges plus GST, and every hallmarked piece must carry its six-digit HUID — easy to misquote and non-compliant on paper.

Who it helps. Small gold and silver jewellers billing by weight with making charges and mandatory hallmarking.

What it does. Maintain pieces by purity, gross/net weight and HUID; at billing, pull the day's metal rate and compute rate times weight plus making (per-gram or flat) plus GST plus hallmarking, then print a compliant tax invoice with the HUID on it; stock stays valued at today's rate.

Regulation. BIS Hallmarking (HUID) mandatory; GST on jewellery (IN)
