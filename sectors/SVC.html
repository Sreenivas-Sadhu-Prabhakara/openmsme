MSME-034

Cheque-Bounce (S.138) Case Clock

Live

Problem. A S.138 NI Act dishonour case runs on a rigid deadline cascade (notice within 30 days of the dishonour memo, drawer gets 15 days to pay, complaint filed within 30 days after that); miss one window and the case dies.

Who it helps. Solo advocates and small litigation firms with cheque-bounce dockets

What it does. Enter the dishonour date; the app auto-computes every downstream deadline, generates the statutory demand notice, and fires WhatsApp/SMS reminders before each window closes.

Regulation. NI Act s.138/142, Limitation Act
