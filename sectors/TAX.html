MSME-027

8% Flat vs Graduated-Rates Decision Tool (PH)

Live

Problem. Every self-employed Filipino and professional must elect early each year between the 8% flat tax on gross over ₱250k and graduated rates plus percentage tax — and most choose blind.

Who it helps. Freelancers, online sellers, professionals and sole proprietors.

MSME-659

GST Common-Credit ITC Reversal Calculator (Rule 42/43)

Idea

Problem. When turnover is a mix of taxable and exempt supply, common input-tax credit must be apportioned and partly reversed every month under Rule 42, with an annual true-up and a 60-month capital-goods reversal under Rule 43. The formula (D1/D2, T4) is error-prone and a wrong figure invites ITC-recovery demands with interest.

Who it helps. Small manufacturers, traders, clinics, builders and agri-traders making both taxable and exempt/nil-rated supplies.

What it does. Enter total ITC, exempt vs taxable turnover and the capital-goods list; it computes the monthly Rule 42 reversal, the Rule 43 capital-goods reversal over 60 months and the year-end true-up, producing an auditable working with the exact GSTR-3B Table 4(B) figures to key in.

Regulation. CGST Rules 42 & 43

MSME-660

Rule 37 Unpaid-Supplier ITC Reversal & Reclaim Tracker

Idea

Problem. If a buyer does not pay a supplier within 180 days of invoice, the ITC already claimed must be reversed with interest under the second proviso to Sec 16(2)/Rule 37, and reclaimed only once paid. Almost no micro-firm tracks the 180-day clock per invoice and they get caught in audit.

Who it helps. MSMEs that stretch supplier payments and claim ITC on purchases — trading, construction and job-work chains.

What it does. Import the purchase register; it runs a 180-day countdown per invoice, flags credits due for reversal, computes the interest, generates the GSTR-3B reversal entry and auto-reinstates the credit in the month payment is recorded.

Regulation. CGST Sec 16(2) second proviso & Rule 37

MSME-661

QRMP Optimizer & IFF B2B Upload Helper

Idea

Problem. QRMP taxpayers must decide each month whether to pay via the fixed-sum 35% challan or self-assessment, and whether to push B2B invoices into the Invoice Furnishing Facility in months 1 and 2 so buyers get ITC. Most either overpay the 35% challan or strand their buyers' credit by skipping IFF.

Who it helps. Small GST taxpayers with turnover up to Rs 5 crore on the Quarterly Return Monthly Payment scheme.

What it does. Enter the quarter's sales and last period's liability; it recommends the monthly PMT-06 amount (fixed-sum vs self-assessment), flags which B2B invoices to file in IFF within the Rs 50-lakh cap, and lays out the interest-safe GSTR-1/3B quarterly calendar.

Regulation. GST QRMP scheme / Invoice Furnishing Facility (IFF) / Form PMT-06

MSME-662

194Q vs 206C(1H) Buyer/Seller TDS-TCS Decider & Ledger

Idea

Problem. On high-value goods the law overlaps: the buyer may owe 0.1% TDS under Sec 194Q while the seller may owe 0.1% TCS under Sec 206C(1H), and 194Q takes precedence. Both sides routinely double-tax or miss it, then fight 26AS mismatches.

Who it helps. Traders, manufacturers and wholesalers with any customer or supplier crossing Rs 50 lakh of goods a year.

What it does. Enter the counterparty's turnover bracket and the running value of goods traded; it decides who must deduct/collect, from which rupee the Rs 50-lakh threshold bites, the correct rate (incl. no-PAN 5%/1%), and keeps a per-party ledger with the amount to deduct on the next invoice.

Regulation. Income-tax Sec 194Q & Sec 206C(1H)

MSME-663

GST Place-of-Supply & IGST-vs-CGST/SGST Determiner

Idea

Problem. Charging IGST when it should be CGST+SGST (or vice-versa) is one of the commonest GST errors and is hard to undo — the buyer loses ITC and the seller faces re-payment. Rules differ for goods, services, bill-to-ship-to, transport, events and OIDAR.

Who it helps. Freelancers, service firms and traders invoicing across state lines, and anyone handling bill-to/ship-to orders.

What it does. A short wizard (supply type, supplier state, recipient/registration, delivery or performance location) applies the IGST Act Sec 10-13 rules to return the place of supply and whether to charge IGST or CGST+SGST, with the governing section cited for the invoice file.

Regulation. IGST Act Sec 10-13 (place of supply)
