MSME-047

Senior/PWD Discount Compliance Till

Idea

Problem. PH law mandates a 20% discount AND VAT exemption for seniors and PWDs computed off the VAT-net base; businesses routinely miscompute it (overcharging customers or losing the tax deduction) and must keep a substantiating logbook for BIR.

Who it helps. Restaurants, caterers, homestays, small hotels and tour operators serving walk-in guests.

What it does. Enter gross price and headcount; it strips the 12% VAT, applies the 20% discount in the correct sequence, prints a compliant line-itemised receipt, and keeps the mandated digital discount logbook with BIR-ready monthly totals.

Regulation. RA 9994 (Senior Citizens), RA 10754 (PWD), BIR VAT rules

MSME-734

Boat Passenger Manifest & Coast Guard Logger

Idea

Problem. The Philippine Coast Guard requires a signed passenger manifest before every departure; operators scribble names on paper, can't prove headcount vs authorised capacity or life-vest counts, and lose the records they need after an incident or audit.

Who it helps. Island-hopping, ferry, bangka and dive-boat operators carrying tourists.

What it does. Offline-first phone/tablet app to build a per-trip manifest (passenger name, age, nationality, address, contact, fare), check boarded count against the vessel's authorised capacity and life-vest count, capture the captain's sign-off, print/share the PCG-format manifest, and keep a searchable trip log with daily and per-vessel summaries, all without a connection at the dock.

Regulation. Philippine Coast Guard passenger-manifest requirement; MARINA small-vessel rules

MSME-735

Hotel Room GST Rate & Tax-Invoice Helper

Idea

Problem. Post-2025 rules confuse owners over which room sells at which GST slab, whether to opt into 'specified premises', transaction-value vs declared-tariff, and how to invoice advances, so invoices carry the wrong rate and ITC is lost or disputed.

Who it helps. Small and mid hotels, resorts and homestays registered under GST.

What it does. Enter room rate, discounts and whether the property is a specified premises; it returns the correct GST rate and tax treatment, generates a SAC-9963 compliant tax invoice (and advance receipt) with the right CGST/SGST split, and keeps a yearly rate-decision log for the auditor.

Regulation. GST on hotel accommodation (SAC 9963; 'specified premises' declaration; transaction-value basis)

MSME-736

Contactless Guest Check-In & Digital Guest Register

Idea

Problem. Front desks still fill paper guest registers and photocopy IDs; the register is illegible, unsearchable, and a liability when police or municipal inspectors ask for a specific date's entries or a named guest's details.

Who it helps. Small hotels, lodges, guesthouses and homestays (5-40 rooms) that must keep a police-inspectable guest register.

What it does. Foreign guests are flagged and their data pre-filled for the separate Form C hand-off.

Regulation. State police lodging/guest-register rules (Sarais Act 1867 where applicable); Form C hand-off under Registration of Foreigners Rules
