FIRST BUILD WAVE

The first open-source builds for MSMEs

These 15 tools go out ahead of the rest of the catalog — chosen to cover many sectors, favour fast-to-ship stacks, and solve the sharpest problems first. Each ships as its own open-source repository and its own explainer site. Status below is honest: the ones marked Live are released and usable today; the rest are still being built.

MSME-001 · Manufacturing & engineering

Job-Work ITC-04 Reconciler

jobwork-itc04-reconciler

Go service: register inbound/outbound job-work challans per principal, run the 1yr/3yr deemed-supply aging clock, reconcile parts, and export portal-ready ITC-04 JSON.

Why first: Rank-1 candidate and an instant-pay compliance pain: getting the deemed-supply deadline or the ITC-04 file wrong is a direct GST demand, and a spreadsheet literally cannot compute it. Go single-binary ships fast. Opens the MFG sector.

MSME-002 · Manufacturing & engineering

Machining Quote Calculator

machining-quote-calculator

Zero-login static calculator: material + machine rate/hr + cycle/setup time + qty + scrap% + margin -> per-piece price with transparent breakdown and printable PDF quote.

Why first: Rank-2 and the fastest possible launch (static/S, offline, no backend). Turns a 20-minute gut guess into a 2-minute defensible quote every machine shop makes daily. Distinct from the ITC-04 reconciler so the two MFG tools don't overlap.

MSME-006 · Textiles, apparel & handloom

Textile Unit-Costing Calculator

fabric-saree-costing

Static per-meter/per-piece costing: yarn count + warp/weft consumption + wastage% + job-work (dye/print/embroidery) + labour/overhead -> true cost, break-even and margin bands.

Why first: Rank-4; encodes the sector's real yarn-count->consumption cost model a blank spreadsheet can't, catching the wastage and job-work costs textile MSMEs always miss. Static = instant launch. Opens TEX.

MSME-007 · Textiles, apparel & handloom

Yarn Inventory by Count, Lot & Shade

yarn-inventory-count-lot

Go app: track yarn by count (e.g. 2/60s), colour/dye-lot and supplier; record issue/receipt, compute consumption against production, and alert on reorder points.

Why first: Rank-5; models yarn the way the trade actually buys it (count + dye-lot) so shade continuity and reorder alerts work where generic stock apps fail. Functionally distinct from the TEX costing calc, justifying a second high-ranked TEX pick.

MSME-008 · Food processing & F&B

FSSAI License Navigator

fssai-license-navigator

Go decision-tree: turnover + activity + capacity + states + export status -> exact FSSAI licence tier, document checklist, govt fee, plus an expiry watchdog that nags before renewal.

Why first: Rank-6; the gateway compliance step for every food business, replacing Rs 3-10k consultant guesswork and lapsed-licence penalties. The schedule-wise eligibility logic is beyond any spreadsheet. go/S launches quickly; opens FOOD.

MSME-011 · Food processing & F&B

Aggregator Payout Reconciler

aggregator-payout-reconciler

Go reconciler: import the aggregator settlement CSV + order export, match order-by-order, flag commission/levy overcharges, and show true per-order and per-dish margin net of commission, packaging and GST/VAT.

Why first: Rank-9; no incumbent shows net-of-everything margin, so it recovers the silent leakage every Swiggy/Zomato/foodpanda seller suffers. A money-recovery tool, functionally distinct from the FSSAI navigator, so the two FOOD picks don't overlap.

MSME-015 · Retail, kirana & distribution

BoliKhata — Voice Credit Ledger

bolikhata-voice-ledger

Offline-first Flutter app: owner taps once and speaks 'Ramu, 200, cooking oil' in Hindi/Tamil/Tagalog/Cebuano; on-device speech-to-text posts the credit-ledger entry and balances sync when signal returns.

Why first: Rank-11 and the strongest open-source statement in the wave: the true OSS, offline-first, vernacular-voice answer to closed, data-locked Khatabook/OKCredit, so a non-typing owner actually keeps it current. Our single flutter pick, well under the 3-cap.

MSME-022 · Exports & trade compliance

Export Price Builder (EXW to DDP)

Static builder from ex-works cost up through inland haulage, THC, freight, insurance (CIF+10%), bank/LC charges and margin -> the correct FOB/CFR/CIF/DDP number per Incoterm with a shareable breakdown.

Why first: Rank-15; encodes the Incoterm logic and the CIF+10% insurance rule a spreadsheet hides, yielding a defensible itemized export quote in minutes. Static/S opens the high-value EXP sector with the fastest stack.

MSME-027 · Accounting, GST/BIR & compliance

8% Flat vs Graduated-Rates Decision Tool (PH)

GSTR-2B vs Purchase ITC Reconciler
